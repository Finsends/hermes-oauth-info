Private personal integration

Hermes Personal Assistant

A personal desktop integration for scheduling, calendar review and email summaries.

What it does

The integration connects the owner's Google Calendar and Gmail account to the owner's Hermes assistant. It reads relevant calendars to support planning and can create, update or delete calendar events when the owner authorizes scheduling. Gmail access is read-only and supports requested email summaries.

Personal use only

This informational website is public. The assistant itself is not offered as a public service: there is no registration, download, hosted assistant or Google sign-in on this website. Visiting this page does not grant access to any Google account or data.

The Google connection belongs to the owner. Credentials and private records are not included in this website or its source repository.
