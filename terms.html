Use information

Hermes Personal Assistant is a private personal integration. It is not a public product, subscription or hosted service, and it is not offered for third-party registration or use.

This website only explains the integration and its data practices. It does not request Google authorization, collect payment or provide access to the assistant.

Google access requires the account owner's consent. Calendar modifications must follow the owner's instructions. The owner can stop using the integration and revoke its Google access at any time.

This personal integration is independently maintained and is not a Google product or a public distribution of Hermes software.
