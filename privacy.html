Data handling

Privacy notice

This notice covers the private Google integration described on this website. It is for its owner's personal use.

Data access and purpose

With the owner's authorization, the integration reads Google Calendar data and creates, updates or deletes events for personal planning. It reads Gmail messages and metadata for requested summaries. Gmail access is read-only and does not allow sending or modifying mail.

Processing and retention

Retrieved data and responses may be retained according to application settings. Relevant content may be processed by configured AI service providers to answer requests. Processing, retention and deletion depend on the application and provider settings.

Use and disclosure

Google data is used for the owner's requested features. It is not sold, used for advertising or published on this website.

Control and deletion

The owner can revoke access in Google Account connections. Revocation stops future authorized access but does not erase previously retained data or undo earlier calendar changes. Retained copies must be removed separately through the application, storage or provider controls that apply.

This website

This informational website contains no Google sign-in, forms, analytics scripts or cookies added by the site. GitHub Pages hosts these pages and may process request information under its privacy statement.

Updates

This notice will be updated when data practices change.
